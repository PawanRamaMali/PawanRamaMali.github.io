01 / About

I build clinical software that ships and holds up under audit.

In short

Ten years building clinical data platforms and machine learning systems for pharmaceutical and biotech teams. I still write the code that goes to production, not only the plan for it.

Clinical dataLLM & agentsR / PythonCDISCMLOps
In practice

Most of it is retrieval systems and agents over patient-level clinical data. Every answer cites the record it came from, sits behind guardrails, and reaches a human reviewer before anyone acts on it. The stack is Python, R, SQL and PostgreSQL on AWS, shipped with Docker and CI/CD.

Underneath sits the clinical detail: CDISC SDTM and ADaM, legacy SAS rebuilt as validated R, risk-based quality monitoring and biomarker analytics, all under GxP. I also build in the open. I founded R4SUB, a seven-package ecosystem for submission readiness, and have authored ten R packages, nine of them on CRAN.
